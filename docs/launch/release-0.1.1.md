# Your words. A journal. — 0.1.1

Start with a working Markdown guide, choose a journal and compose. The guide itself becomes the sample output.

- **Open sample manuscript** in English or Korean, with original figures, tables, notes and references. Existing edits are preserved.
- **Journal of Achmage** in deep navy and **Journal of Command & Space** in teal and pink, with vector PDF logos and portable template ZIPs.
- English/Korean controls for the principal workflow; expanded, display-aware PDF preview.
- Actual bilingual video demonstrations, 15-second teasers, captions and five gallery images per language.

[Watch in English](https://youtu.be/cMVZ1uaMRdk) · [한국어 시연](https://youtu.be/ObFFGrG0EB4)

## Choose a download

- `achmage-academic-editor-ultra-0.1.1.zip`: plugin only. Extract into `<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/achmage-academic-editor-ultra/`, reload Obsidian and enable it.
- `aaeu-working-samples-0.1.1.zip`: both Markdown guides, image assets, both brands' PDF and AF outputs, portable templates and vector logos.
- `aaeu-launch-media-0.1.1.zip`: English/Korean videos, subtitles, gallery images, thumbnails and music credit. Videos are also individually downloadable.
- Individual brand PDFs, AF packages and template ZIPs are available below.

Requires Obsidian 1.8.9+. Independent of HanMark. Not yet listed in the Obsidian Community directory. Native AF export requires desktop. Fonts are discovered locally and are not distributed.

## Validation and limits

122 tests and local build, type, lint, bundle, Community and release checks passed. The isolated Obsidian run produced four-page outputs for each language and each brand, with no captured runtime errors. Both wordmarks and their exported first pages retain vector artwork. Browser engine/OCR and repeated preview resizing were checked. Production dependency audit reported zero vulnerabilities at verification time. Windows, macOS and Linux CI runs are attached to this source commit; macOS hardware was not tested.

Reference candidates require human review. The AF demonstration validates the supported sample features, not every possible Affinity document; check the final text frame after edits. Affinity edits do not sync back to Markdown.
